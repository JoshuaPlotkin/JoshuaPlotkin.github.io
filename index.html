About the Lab

The Plotkin Lab at Stony Brook University studies the organization, function, and plasticity of basal ganglia circuits to understand how neural activity shapes behavior in health and disease. We combine electrophysiology, advanced imaging, circuit manipulation, and behavioral approaches to investigate the mechanisms underlying neurological and psychiatric disorders.
